🌟 LearnPlay Kids

An interactive, child-safe educational gaming platform designed for Pre-K through Grade 6+ students. Practice arithmetic, phonics, grammar, typing, logic, place value, geography, and earth science.

👩‍🏫 For Parents & Teachers

All 10 learning games dynamically adapt to Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty modes. Progress is saved privately in your browser's local storage with zero ads, zero external tracking, and no account required.

♿ Accessibility & Controls

Supports keyboard navigation, on-screen touch controls, optional Web Speech API read-aloud prompts, and gentle, positive reinforcement for young learners.
